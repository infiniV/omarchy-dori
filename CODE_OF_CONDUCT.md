# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise the code, not
the person who wrote it.

## What is not welcome

Harassment, personal attacks, insults tied to who someone is, sexualised
language or imagery, publishing someone's private information, and sustained
disruption of a discussion. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, and anywhere else this project is being discussed.

## Enforcement

Report anything that crosses the line to the maintainer at
<rushamelahi26@gmail.com>. Reports stay private.

The maintainer will do whatever is needed and proportionate: edit or delete the
content, explain why, and where it keeps happening, block the account from the
repository. There is no appeals committee — this is a one-person project, and
that is the whole process.

## Scope

This applies to everyone taking part, including the maintainer.
